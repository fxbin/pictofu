# PicToFu Distribution Launch Kit #01

Status: launch-ready copy + creative plan  
Parent: #119 / #116 / #65  
Audience: overseas, mobile-first photobooth users  
Core promise: **No install. Open. Pose. Download.**

## Test design

Batch 01 deliberately tests three creative angles instead of flooding channels:

1. **Outcome-first** — finished strip appears immediately.
2. **Process-first** — Open → Pose → Review → Download.
3. **Use-case-first** — date / best friends / graduation context.

Primary product metrics:

- landing → `start_booth`
- landing → `export_completed`
- `share_clicked / export_completed`
- referral sessions from the new `Make yours` loop

No paid ads in Batch 01. No new thin landing pages.

---

## Pinterest production contract

Use a vertical **2:3 canvas** (target 1000×1500) for the first static Pin set. Keep the actual PicToFu strip large and readable, with only a short top hook and small bottom CTA/brand treatment.

Recommended composition:

- top 15–20%: one short hook
- middle 60–70%: approved human demo asset, unmodified except fit/shadow/background
- bottom 15–20%: `Make yours on PicToFu` + `No install`
- always preserve the real preset geometry; do not crop a 1×4 strip into a fake grid

## Short-form vertical video contract

Master canvas: **9:16**. Build one clean master per concept and adapt copy/CTA per channel rather than editing a completely different product story for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

Rules:

- show the finished output in the first 1–2 seconds
- keep the product UI truthful
- use screen recording for the browser flow plus approved demo outcome
- keep text away from extreme top/bottom UI zones
- no fake “post directly to TikTok/Instagram” interaction
- CTA is `Make yours on PicToFu`

### Concept A — “This is a website?”

**Intent:** process-first / broad online-photobooth acquisition  
**Landing:** `/online-photobooth`  
**Master duration:** 12–15s

Storyboard:

| Time | Visual | On-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1.5s | Finished Classic Booth strip fills screen | `This is a website.` |
| 1.5–3s | Browser opens PicToFu | `No photobooth app.` |
| 3–6s | Choose Classic → camera countdown | `Open → pose` |
| 6–9s | Review one frame / small reframe gesture | `Fix the frame you want` |
| 9–12s | Style & Export → finished PNG | `Download the whole strip` |
| 12–15s | Finished asset + brand | `Make yours on PicToFu ✦` |

Caption A1: `A tiny photobooth that lives in your browser ✦ No install. Open, pose, download.`  
Caption A2: `Four poses → one little photo strip. Made in the browser with PicToFu.`

Tracked links:

- TikTok: `/online-photobooth?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=demo_launch_01&utm_content=classic-process`
- Instagram: `/online-photobooth?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=demo_launch_01&utm_content=classic-process`
