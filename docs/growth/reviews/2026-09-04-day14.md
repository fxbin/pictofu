# PicToFu Day 14 Growth Review — 2026-09-04

## Window

First-party aggregate evidence from `2026-08-19` through `2026-09-03` UTC.

## Decision

**ITERATE**

PicToFu has crossed the earliest validation threshold where a cold search visitor can reach the core value outcome, but traffic is still too small and partially contaminated to justify scaling or a feature expansion cycle.

## Evidence quality

Confidence: **directional / low-volume**

Caveats:
- `landing_view` is a configured product-landing stage reach, not site-wide UV.
- early direct-to-Booth sessions can lack acquisition context.

- retention is consent-dependent and sparse.
- before #295, Upload emitted `capture_completed` for any non-empty prepared batch while Camera emitted it only after a complete camera set; pre-fix source-level capture counts are therefore not directly comparable.

## Completion measurement correction — Upload

Historical capture-source rows were:

| Source | Pre-fix `capture_completed` | Exports |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| camera | 34 | 6 |
| upload | 7 | 0 |

Code audit after this review found that the denominator was asymmetric:
- Camera counted `capture_completed` only after the full camera sequence was ready.
- Upload counted `capture_completed` after any non-empty upload batch, including a partial set that could not yet export for the selected layout.

Therefore **`upload: 7 → 0` must not be interpreted as an Upload→Export conversion rate or as evidence that seven export-ready upload sessions failed**.

#295 corrects the measurement contract so `capture_completed` means the current photo set first satisfies the selected layout's required photo count. After that correction reaches Production, collect a fresh window before making a source-level Camera vs Upload completion claim.

The Upload product path may still deserve real-device observation, but the Day 14 aggregate data does not establish an Upload product bug.

## Gate rationale

### Why not CONTINUE / scale
- search evidence is promising but tiny;
- only one clean search-attributed export is observed;
- owner/assistant contamination is material relative to total traffic;
- source-level completion measurement needs a fresh post-#295 window before Camera vs Upload comparison.

### Why not HOLD
- cold search visitors are reaching Start, Capture, and Export;
- search-attributed landings accelerated at the end of the window;
- the core product can produce completed strips in production.

### Why ITERATE
There is enough real behavior to keep the thesis alive, but the next cycle should improve measurement truth and validate completion quality rather than expand feature breadth.

## Next actions

1. Keep canonical SEO URLs and information architecture stable for another observation window.
2. Obtain Search Console impressions/clicks/CTR/query/page evidence before adding new SEO intent pages.
3. Ship #295 through the normal `dev → main` release boundary, then collect a fresh Camera/Upload completion window.
4. Observe Upload→Review→Export on desktop and mobile without assuming the historical zero-export denominator proves a defect.
5. Keep framing/crop/ratio behavior under observation; treat high Pan/Ratio usage as ambiguous evidence.
6. Keep social experiments bounded; do not make social the primary growth bet yet.
7. Re-run the same weekly query pack and compare clean search + post-fix completion evidence.
